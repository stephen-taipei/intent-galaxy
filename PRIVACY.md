# Privacy Policy — Intent Galaxy browser extension

_Last updated: 2026-10-19_ · [繁體中文](#隱私權政策--intent-galaxy-瀏覽器擴充功能)

This policy covers the Intent Galaxy extension for Chrome and Firefox (“the extension”). The npm package and CLI run on your own computer and are not covered by any online service.

## Summary

- The extension does **not** send any data to the developer or to any third party. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.
- Everything the extension reads or saves stays in your browser on your device.
- The extension does not load or run code from the internet.

## What the extension reads

Only on the supported AI chat sites (claude.ai, chatgpt.com, grok.com, gemini.google.com, chat.deepseek.com, chat.qwen.ai, www.perplexity.ai, copilot.microsoft.com, chat.mistral.ai, www.kimi.com), and only to do its job:

- The question you type in the chat box, so it can prepare a prompt for you.
- The AI’s reply in the conversation, so it can draw the options panel.

When you click a button in the panel, the extension places text into the site’s chat box. You decide whether to send it. Anything you send goes to that AI site under **that site’s own** privacy policy, exactly as if you had typed it yourself.

## What the extension saves

Saved with the browser’s extension storage, on your device only:

- Your question, the options the AI suggested, and what you picked, so the panel can be restored when you reopen the same conversation. Up to 100 records, each kept for up to 30 days.
- Panel preferences such as size, position, opacity and language.

Nothing is saved in private/incognito windows or in ChatGPT temporary chats. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

## Optional local connection

If you choose to run the Intent Galaxy command-line tool on your own computer, the panel can talk to it at `http://127.0.0.1:4787`. This address only reaches your own computer; nothing leaves your device. If the tool isn’t running, nothing is sent.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and noted in the extension’s release notes before it takes effect.
Questions: support@stephen.taipei · <https://github.com/stephen-taipei/intent-galaxy/issues>

# 隱私權政策 — Intent Galaxy 瀏覽器擴充功能

_最後更新：2026-10-19_

本政策適用於 Chrome 與 Firefox 版 Intent Galaxy 擴充功能（以下稱「本擴充功能」）。

## 重點

- 本擴充功能**不會**把任何資料傳給開發者或任何第三方。沒有帳號、沒有統計分析、沒有追蹤、沒有廣告。
- 本擴充功能讀取或儲存的內容，全部留在你裝置上的瀏覽器裡。
- 本擴充功能不會從網路下載或執行程式碼。

## 會讀取什麼

只在支援的 AI 聊天網站（claude.ai、chatgpt.com、grok.com、gemini.google.com、chat.deepseek.com、chat.qwen.ai、www.perplexity.ai、copilot.microsoft.com、chat.mistral.ai、www.kimi.com），而且只為了完成功能：

- 你在對話框輸入的問題，用來幫你準備提示詞。
- 對話中 AI 的回覆，用來畫出選項面板。

你在面板按下按鈕時，本擴充功能會把文字放進網站的對話框，要不要送出由你決定。你送出的內容會交給該 AI 網站，適用**該網站自己的**隱私權政策，跟你自己打字送出完全一樣。

## 會儲存什麼

存在瀏覽器的擴充功能儲存空間，只在你的裝置上：

- 你的問題、AI 給的選項和你的選擇，讓你重新打開同一個對話時能還原面板。最多 100 筆，每筆最多保留 30 天。
- 面板偏好設定，例如大小、位置、透明度和語言。

無痕視窗與 ChatGPT 暫時對話不會儲存任何內容。移除本擴充功能會一併刪除以上資料。

## 選用的本機連線

如果你自己在電腦上執行 Intent Galaxy 命令列工具，面板可以連到 `http://127.0.0.1:4787` 與它溝通。這個位址只會連到你自己的電腦，資料不會離開你的裝置。沒有執行該工具時，不會傳送任何東西。

## 政策變更與聯絡方式

政策若有變更，會先在此頁公告並寫進擴充功能的版本說明，再生效。
問題請寄 support@stephen.taipei，或到 <https://github.com/stephen-taipei/intent-galaxy/issues>。
